* Websites: brand sites, product landing pages, personal homepages, portfolios, campaign pages, blogs, and content sites.

* Product prototypes and demos: MVPs, interactive prototypes, proofs of concept, or product demos that show a core function and user flow.

3. Follow and tag the official account. Follow @AutoClawAIer on X and mention the account in your post.
4. Submit the campaign form. Provide your X post link and the email address registered to your AutoClaw account: Open the submission form.
